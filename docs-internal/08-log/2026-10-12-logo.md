# 2026-10-12 · Logo

## What happened
- **Exploration:** 16 logo rounds across geometric, dimensional, liquid, literal-UI and FewTone-derived marks. ADR 0006 lists each direction and why it was rejected.
- **Decision:** the owner chose to use the FewTone studio mark exactly as it is. The owner added the source files to `apps/www/public/Logo/`.
- **The name:** it is written "FewTone UI" in the logo, as everywhere else. The code-style `fewtone/ui` is retired; ADR 0004 has a note.
- **Motion:** two rounds of logo animation were built on the logo sheet:
  - pieces assembling, a half-turn loader, and the halves parting on hover
  - liquid moving inside the channel: pour, bloom, ripple, flow, fill

  The owner decided the logo needs no animation, so it stays static.
- **In the site:**
  - `logo.tsx` has two components: `Logo` (horizontal: mark and "FewTone UI") and `LogoMark` (the mark alone, an inline SVG in `currentColor`).
  - The header uses `Logo`.
  - The Navy footer uses the stacked version: the mark above the name.
  - The Lab tokens page shows all three versions.
  - The mark now sits closer to the name than before.
  - New tokens `--size-logo-sm` and `--size-logo-lg`.
  - Icons: `app/favicon.ico` (studio favicon), `app/icon.svg` (follows the browser's light or dark scheme) and `app/apple-icon.png` (180 px).
- **Fixes:**
  - Biome no longer lints `.svg` image assets. They are loaded as images, so the `<title>` rule doesn't apply.
- **Cleanup:** deleted the exploration page `01-brand/explorations/logo-round-01.html`, following the convention.

- **Theme toggle:** fixed an older hydration mismatch.
  - Cause: the `aria-label` depended on the theme, which only the browser knows.
  - Fix: a neutral label until the page has loaded.
  - The "1 Issue" badge in the dev overlay is gone.
