Design system · Foundations · Decided 8 Oct 2026

Colour and type, defined once

The system so far: a brand colour with a full 12-step scale, a pure neutral scale for everything else, four status colours, the semantic tokens components will use, and typography with a type scale. Decided: Navy as the brand colour, Geist and Geist Mono for type, and status colours in Navy’s deep, muted tone. This page is kept as the exploration record. The implemented system is specified in docs-internal/01-brand/design-system.md and lives in packages/ui/src/styles.

2 · Neutrals

Pure black, white and grey

Everything outside the brand moments uses this scale. It has no tint, so the site reads as plain near-black or white. Steps follow the Radix model: backgrounds, interactive surfaces, borders, solids and text, each with a fixed job.

3 · Status colours

Success, warning, error, info

Components need these for alerts, form errors and toasts. Draft 2 makes them calmer: about a third less saturated than before, so they inform without shouting and sit comfortably next to the neutral site. They follow the same 12-step recipe as the brand colour and never appear as decoration.

5 · Typography · Decided

Geist and Geist Mono

One family for everything, with its matching mono. Free under the SIL Open Font License, so it ships in the MIT repo. We will self-host the files from the geist npm package rather than Google Fonts, because only that build includes the stylistic alternates. Because Geist is also Vercel's typeface, the brand colour, layout and motion carry our identity.

6 · Type scale

Sizes, spacing and weights

Fixed sizes for all UI and docs text. Only display and h1 grow with the screen. Letter-spacing tightens as size grows. The values follow Vercel's own Geist scale: about −0.05em at display sizes, −0.04em at 32px, −0.02em at 20px, and none for body text.

My lean: Midnight leads, with Clay as the warm highlight. Midnight on its own is beautifully quiet, but deep navy in a dark theme sits close to the near-black background. A small amount of Clay on "New" badges and the announcement dot gives the brand a warm spark where it matters. If you want a single colour, Navy is the Midnight variation that holds up best in dark mode, and Sienna is the Clay variation that stays clearest of the error red.
