How the best dark-first brands use one colour

I pulled the live CSS from about 38 product sites. The ones below get one hue, glow and grain right. Each tile is a small rebuild of the technique, not a screenshot.

Rim light

Near-black with one light source behind an object. Grain only shows where there is light.

  • VercelWhite rim glow on black, 70px noise tile in multiply at 0.42.
  • LinearGlow at only 4% white, eased stops, grain per card.
  • ResendSatin light streaks; a separate hero image per theme.

Accent glow

The brand colour appears as light: a white-hot core, then light hue, then the brand, then nothing.

  • ClerkWhite 10% → light violet 30% → brand 50% → transparent.
  • RaycastRed glow keycaps; pill border runs light pink to dark maroon.
  • SupabaseGreen spotlight from the top edge at 15%.

Tonal field

The whole surface is the hue, running light to dark of itself, with visible grain on top.

  • MercurySVG fractal noise (0.65, 3 octaves) at 0.2, faded out by a gradient.
  • ArcFlat slate and deep blue with a noise tile. Matte with no gradient.
  • ZedNoise at 3% in light and 1.2% in dark; dithering against banding.

Theme-aware grain

Noise used as a mask over a colour that flips with the theme, so grain never turns muddy in either mode.

  • NeonNoise mask over black in light and white in dark, at 7.5%.
  • MintlifyOne green, two lightness values: darker in light mode, brighter in dark.
  • Paco CourseyFixed craft-grain overlay from SVG fractal noise.
What we take from the research

Rules every option follows

One number drives the palette

A hue angle in OKLCH. Neutrals carry the same hue at almost no chroma, so greys feel related to the brand.

Gradients change lightness, not hue

Light, solid and dark stops of one hue. Chroma peaks at the solid step, so midpoints never turn grey.

Glow belongs to dark mode

In light mode, glow becomes a pale wash of the hue plus tinted shadows. Additive light on white reads as a smudge.

Brand shifts per theme

The solid colour is a little darker in light mode and a little brighter in dark, like Mintlify.

Grain is a mask, not a filter

A 200px static noise tile masks a white layer in dark and a black layer in light. About 4–6% visible. Never animated.

Near-blacks are tinted

The darkest background carries the hue at very low chroma instead of pure black, like Linear and Cursor.
