Four ways to wear a matte gradient

Each direction below keeps the same signature: a soft gradient field under a fine grain, finished matte instead of glossy. What changes is the palette, the type, the shape and how things move. Every direction is shown in light and dark, with a hero, two cards, buttons and toggles.

The shared signature

How the matte surface is built

Four layers, stacked in this order. They become tokens and one .surface-matte utility, so every component gets the same finish in both themes. Turn layers off to see what each one adds.

Palette
Surface, matte Gradient · grain · top light · edge

  1. Two or three OKLCH stops, blended as soft radial blobs or a heat-tint band. OKLCH keeps the midpoints from turning muddy grey.

  2. A tiled SVG noise blended over the gradient. This is what makes it matte: it breaks up the smooth sheen and hides colour banding.

  3. A 1px inner highlight on the top edge. It reads as light catching a physical edge, without any glossy reflection.

  4. A low-contrast inner border plus a soft drop shadow. Keeps the surface separate from the page in light mode, where gradients wash out.

Your call

What I need from you

A direction, or a mix

Pick a letter, or combine parts, for example A palette with C type or B but with A's motion. You can also ask for another round in one direction.

A name, or more options

Pick one from the table, suggest your own and I'll check it, or ask for a new batch in a different style, such as invented words.

What happens next

I write the chosen direction into docs-internal/01-brand/ as colour, type, shape and motion tokens, record the decision, and keep this page as the exploration record.

My lean: A, Anodized, named anodize. It is the furthest from the reference libraries, the heat-tint band is easy to recognise, and it has the cleanest availability, including the bare npm name. If it feels too cold, borrowing B's speckle grain and weighted springs would warm it up without losing the identity.
