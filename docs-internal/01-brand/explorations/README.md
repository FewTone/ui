## Brief (from the owner, after round 1)
- One brand colour, two at most.
- Gradients of that hue only: light to dark, or light into glow.
- "Matte finish" means visible grain/noise over the gradient.
- Dark-first, but light mode must be designed too.
- Ground options in researched, real-world references.

## Confirmed choices (round 3, revision 3, 2026-10-08)
- Liked and kept: no grain; "Deeper" one-tone gradient (fixed hue and chroma, ΔL 0.11) on accent surfaces; headline gradient text (full ink at top, softer grey toward the bottom); gradient-edged announcement badge and Pro badge.
- Removed: all glows (hero accent glow, Pro card outer glow, footer upward glow) and the light-leak line at the top of the hero.
- Still open: accent hue, finish level for the remaining details (Minimal / Balanced / Rich), name.

## Brief additions (after round 2)
- The app and site are very minimal: plain near-black (dark) or white (light), neutral UI.
- The brand colour is not the primary colour. It appears only where it means something: footer, Pro/premium areas, select highlights.
- Gradients must read as one consistent colour: fixed hue and chroma, small lightness change.
- Grain: much less, or none.
