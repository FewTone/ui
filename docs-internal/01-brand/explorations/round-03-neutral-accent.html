Neutral first, colour on purpose

The site is plain near-black in dark mode and white in light mode. The brand colour is not the main colour. It appears only in the footer, Pro and premium areas, and small highlights like a "New" badge. Following your notes, grain and all glows are gone, the hero line is removed, and the accent surfaces use the deeper one-tone gradient. What stays from the premium layer: the headline that softens toward the bottom, the gradient-edged badges, the shaded main button, lit card edges, and a lighter pool of colour at the top of the Pro card and footer.

Gradient fix

One tone, gently shaded

Round 2 ran from almost white to almost black, which made the colour jump. Now the hue and chroma stay fixed and only the lightness moves a little, so a surface reads as one colour with soft depth.

Where the colour is allowed

Uses the brand colour

  • Pro and premiumPro plan card, upgrade buttons, Pro badges.
  • "New" signalsBadge on new components, dot in the announcement pill.

Confirmed so far

Deeper one-tone gradient, no grain, no glow, headline gradient, gradient-edged badges.

My lean: Ember with the Balanced finish. On a neutral site a warm accent stands out most in the few places it appears, and Ember stays readable with white text in both themes. If you prefer cool, Cobalt is the most calm and trustworthy.
